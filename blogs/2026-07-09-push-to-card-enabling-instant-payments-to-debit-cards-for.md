Author: Caitlin Steiner, Senior Product Marketing Manager When your operations team needs to disburse funds immediately to cover gig worker payouts, insurance claims or loan disbursements, the payment method you choose determines whether your recipients wait days or have access to their funds in seconds. Push to card enables businesses to send money directly to recipients' debit cards with near real-time access to funds. This capability has become essential in modern business.

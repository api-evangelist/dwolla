The future of lending payments will be defined by companies that successfully integrate real-time payments in lending operations, enabling faster loan funding and immediate loan disbursement as standard offerings rather than premium services. Driven by market forces, regulatory support and technological advancement, the expansion of instant funding in lending will be led by organizations leveraging automation to modernize their operations and propel growth.
